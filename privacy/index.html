Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how contact and visitor data is processed for the 0708 website.

Controller

Contact: contact@0708.nl

What data we collect

  • Contact form fields (name, email, organisation, event details, message, etc.)
  • Server logs and basic technical data (IP address, user agent) for security and debugging
  • Optional analytics cookies if enabled (see below)

Why we collect it

Data is used to respond to enquiries, manage bookings, and maintain the website. Contact form data is forwarded to the site owner and not shared publicly.

Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and future reference for bookings (typically up to 2 years), unless otherwise requested by the user.

Processors

Form delivery is handled via a third-party delivery endpoint (FormSubmit). Where third parties process data, they do so under their privacy terms and the minimum information required to deliver the message.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your contact data by emailing contact@0708.nl. You may also object to processing or request restrictions where applicable.

Cookies & Analytics

Non-essential analytics scripts are disabled by default. If enabled, they are used only to understand site usage and improve the experience; they do not collect personal data beyond technical identifiers.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@0708.nl.
